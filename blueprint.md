# **App Name**: Writing Oasis

## Core Features:

- Benefit Highlights: Landing page showcasing the benefits of "paid online writing jobs.com".
- CTA Buttons: Clear call-to-action buttons linking to the affiliate link.
- Social Proof: Testimonials or success stories from people who have used "paid online writing jobs.com".

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Blue (#0000FF) to evoke trust and professionalism.
- Secondary color: White (#FFFFFF) for clean backgrounds.
- Accent: Black (#000000) to highlight important elements like CTAs.
- Clean and readable sans-serif font for body text.
- Use a single-column layout for easy readability on all devices.
- Use relevant icons to represent the benefits of the service.
